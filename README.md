## What the paper does

1. **Genealogy** — the two goals formed and institutionalized independently (Turing 1950's bracket; Dartmouth's "simulate"; machine consciousness as a separate program since 2003 with its own journals, society, and normative agenda; the 2022–2026 dissociation wave).
2. **Diagnosis of the binding** — four mechanisms: conceptual slippage, narrative structure, commercial incentive, linguistic inertia.
3. **The unbundling postulate (P1–P3)** on a teleological axis, distinct from Searle (metaphysical), Block (property), Chalmers/Butlin (assessment), Seth (epistemological), and Pesonen & Reijula (relevance). Central corollary: the **progress-transfer illusion** — capability progress silently booked as mind progress — explaining over-attribution, milestone personhood panics, "AGI" marketing, and reverse-direction dismissal ("mere statistics").
4. **Consequences** — dual-track governance; full normative responsibility of the engineering goal without a mind verdict; discourse segregation. Plus rejection clauses (R1–R3) and a precedent-search log (Appendix A).
